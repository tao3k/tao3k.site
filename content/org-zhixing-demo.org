* Evidence-native AI lifecycle infrastructure

tao3k turns knowledge, code, scientific models and human records into
admissible action—and turns every action back into searchable, verifiable and
evolvable evidence.

* From intelligence to trusted action

The lifecycle connects structured records, knowledge search, semantic code
understanding, scientific compute, policy admission, typed execution, receipts,
and evidence-governed promotion.

#+begin_example
Record → Understand → Validate → Contract → Admit
       → Execute → Receipt → Evaluate → Promote
#+end_example

* Research and operations form one continuum

Research must not lose its assumptions, provenance, or scientific meaning when
it reaches industrial operation. Operations must return outcomes, failures, and
observations as evidence that research can inspect and improve.

* Built for human agency

AI can move at machine speed. People deserve to grow at human speed. tao3k
automates friction, danger, and repetition while preserving human purpose,
responsibility, the right to understand, and the right to contest high-impact
decisions.
