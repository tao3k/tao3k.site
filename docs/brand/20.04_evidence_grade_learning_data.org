* Purpose

This record defines tao3k's responsibility in the AI learning economy. tao3k does not exist only to consume models, knowledge, code, scientific results, or human records. It must help people and institutions produce higher-quality evidence that can improve future models and future decisions.

This is a main-site doctrine. Product documentation remains authoritative for the mechanisms that implement any part of it.

* Core doctrine

#+begin_quote
Intelligence must earn the right to act. Evidence must earn the right to teach.
#+end_quote

#+begin_quote
Most AI systems consume the world's knowledge. tao3k is built to replenish it.
#+end_quote

#+begin_quote
Produce the evidence that trains what comes next.
#+end_quote

Every qualified action should be able to return as evidence-grade learning data: traceable to its source, validated by contracts, measured for quality, reproducible in context, and governed by human responsibility.

Evidence-grade learning data is the learning and training-data specialization of the generic [[file:../platform/10.01_evidence_native_ai_lifecycle.org][Evidence-native AI Lifecycle]]. It applies the same Intelligence, Action, and Evolution lifecycles to purpose-specific learning eligibility. It is not a parallel lifecycle and does not redefine the platform model.

* Definition

Evidence-grade learning data is not synonymous with a large dataset, a data lake, or a confidence score. It is a versioned knowledge, code, scientific, human, or operational record whose origin, meaning, authority, limits, and observed consequences remain inspectable.

It may support model training and fine-tuning, retrieval and grounding, evaluation and benchmarks, agent qualification, scientific reproduction, human review, or later correction. Its fitness must be assessed for the intended use; no record is universally high quality.

* Evidence-grade quality dimensions

Data does not become evidence-grade merely because it is structured, abundant, or generated by an accepted model. A governed learning record must preserve:

- Provenance :: where, when, by whom, and through which process the record was produced;
- Context :: the problem, environment, purpose, and decision situation;
- Semantics :: claims, entities, relations, constraints, and intended meaning;
- Reproducibility :: the material needed to inspect, rerun, or independently evaluate the result;
- Qualification :: the conditions and proof obligations applied before action;
- Authority :: the source, scope, duration, and revocation conditions of permission;
- Outcome :: what actually occurred rather than only what was proposed or predicted;
- Verification :: how the result supported, weakened, contradicted, or failed to test the original claim;
- Uncertainty :: unresolved questions, confidence limits, contrary evidence, and known failure modes;
- Rights :: ownership, consent, permitted uses, confidentiality, and sharing or training restrictions;
- Versioning :: how the record, claim, or interpretation was corrected, superseded, or withdrawn.

Negative results, human objections, reversals, and failures are first-class learning evidence. Removing them may make a dataset appear cleaner while making future systems less trustworthy.

* Producer commitment

tao3k should help institutions, research teams, engineering organizations, communities, and individuals become producers of trustworthy knowledge and evidence rather than passive consumers of generated intelligence. Every governed action can become a better record; every verified record can improve future knowledge; when rights and purpose permit, that knowledge may improve future training and evaluation.

This commitment does not claim that tao3k already owns a large industrial training corpus. It defines the lifecycle, contracts, quality measures, and rights conditions required to produce higher-quality records over time.

* Evidence Production Lifecycle
This production lifecycle specializes the return path of the Admissibility Circuit for learning, evaluation, retrieval, grounding, and later correction. Each stage inherits the platform lifecycle's evidence, qualification, authority, execution, and receipt boundaries.

** Capture

Preserve the source, author or responsible organization, intent, time, surrounding context, applicable rights, and original representation. Derived records must retain lineage to their inputs.

** Qualify

Apply structural and semantic contracts. Record the authority under which a claim may be used, its uncertainty, its scope, and any unresolved contradiction. Qualification determines admissibility for a specific purpose; it does not manufacture truth.

** Act

When qualified evidence authorizes an operation, preserve the authority envelope, selected inputs, executable plan, runtime receipts, human interventions, and failures. An action without a recoverable context cannot become reliable learning material.

** Verify

Compare intended and observed outcomes. Retain tests, proofs, scientific checks, reproducibility receipts, counter-evidence, and negative results. Failure is evidence and must not be erased merely because it is inconvenient for a narrative or training corpus.

** Return and evolve

Return the verified result to the searchable evidence graph with provenance and version history intact. New evidence may strengthen, narrow, supersede, or invalidate an earlier claim without destroying its history.

** Measure reuse

Measure whether a record improves retrieval, evaluation, training, decision quality, reproducibility, or human understanding. Reuse counts alone do not prove value; downstream utility and harm must remain observable.

* Evidence Passport
An Evidence Passport answers whether a record is inspectable and fit for a declared future use. It may reference its Action Case, Admission Result, Authority Envelope, Runtime Receipts, and Verification Result, but it does not authorize execution and cannot promote evidence into authority.

Passports may describe qualified input evidence or new evidence produced by action. They preserve provenance, contracts, assumptions, uncertainty, rights, human accountability, quality, freshness, counter-evidence, allowed reuse, and purpose-specific retrieval, evaluation, or training eligibility. Not every record is eligible for a Passport, and no Passport is universally valid for every use.

Each reusable record should expose quality dimensions appropriate to its lifecycle stage and intended use.

| Dimension | Question the record must answer |
|-----------+---------------------------------|
| Provenance | Where did it come from, and which transformations produced it? |
| Authority | Who or what authorized the claim, computation, or action? |
| Contract validity | Which structural and semantic requirements were checked? |
| Reproducibility | Can the relevant result be reproduced from preserved context? |
| Freshness | When was it valid, and what could make it stale? |
| Uncertainty | What is unknown, disputed, approximate, or outside scope? |
| Rights | What consent, license, attribution, privacy, or usage limits apply? |
| Human accountability | Which decisions require a named responsible human or institution? |
| Contradictions | Which counterclaims or failed observations remain unresolved? |
| Downstream utility | For which learning or decision task has value been demonstrated? |

These dimensions must not be collapsed into one opaque trust score. A summary indicator may aid navigation, but the underlying receipts and limits remain authoritative.

* Learning surfaces

Evidence-grade learning data may be used for:

- model training, adaptation, and fine-tuning;
- retrieval, semantic search, and grounded generation;
- benchmarks, evaluations, and regression detection;
- agent qualification and action review;
- scientific computation and reproducibility;
- organizational memory and operational learning;
- human inspection, contest, and correction.

The main site describes this shared lifecycle. Individual products document the exact formats, validators, runtimes, graphs, and export mechanisms they actually provide.

* Training permission and data rights

Operational access, service delivery, storage, verification, and model-training permission are distinct authorities. Private evidence, customer records, contributor material, and personal data never acquire training permission merely by entering a tao3k system.

Training or evaluation use requires an explicit purpose, lawful and contractual rights, preserved provenance, applicable consent or license, stated retention and withdrawal rules, and compatibility with the record's sensitivity and authority boundaries. Customers and contributors retain meaningful export, migration, deletion, and exit rights. Shared projections must preserve provenance and use limits rather than laundering governed evidence into unrestricted data exhaust.

* Human value, rights, and responsibility

People must not become an invisible raw material for an AI extraction economy. Knowledge producers, students, researchers, operators, farmers, technical workers, and affected communities must be able to retain attribution, context, applicable rights, and a visible relationship to the value created from their contribution.

High-risk decisions remain subject to human responsibility. A technically reusable record is not automatically ethically or legally admissible for training. Consent, privacy, licensing, safety, and benefit distribution are independent qualification dimensions.

tao3k therefore adopts this commitment:

#+begin_quote
We will not build a one-way AI consumption economy. Every use of intelligence should leave behind better evidence than it consumed.
#+end_quote

* Non-goals

- Accumulating data without a declared purpose or evidence contract.
- Treating model-generated volume as knowledge quality.
- Hiding uncertainty, failed runs, or conflicting evidence to improve a headline metric.
- Selling a universal trust score detached from its receipts.
- Claiming that every tao3k product is itself a training-data product.
- Removing human authorship, rights, responsibility, or benefit from the learning loop.

* Main-site presentation contract

The homepage introduces this doctrine after the Admissibility Circuit, Research to Industry, and Human Agency. It must appear as a specialized outcome of the generic platform lifecycle, not as a second lifecycle or a separate data-platform category. The preferred concise copy is:

#+begin_quote
Most AI systems only consume the world's knowledge. tao3k is built to replenish it. Every qualified action can return as evidence-grade learning data: traceable, contract-checked, reproducible, rights-aware, and measured for downstream value.
#+end_quote

The site must distinguish an established doctrine from currently implemented product capabilities. Capability claims link to the responsible project and its documentation.
