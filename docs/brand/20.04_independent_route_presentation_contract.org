* ACCEPTED Decision
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: independent-route-presentation-contract
:END:

The TAO Three K main site separates brand presentation from route-owned
explanation.  A shared header and company footer establish identity; they do
not authorize shared page bodies.

The homepage is the initial presentation surface.  It states the position,
shows the evidence-to-action idea, communicates the long-term commitment, and
provides deliberate exits.  It must not concatenate abbreviated copies of the
Platform, Products, Solutions, Comparison, Principles, or Roadmap pages.

* ACCEPTED Shared Shell Boundary
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: shared-shell-boundary
:END:

The shared shell owns only:

- brand identity and primary navigation;
- global accessibility and responsive behavior;
- company-level footer information;
- links to contact, field notes, source repositories, and public routes.

Color mode belongs to the primary header.  It is represented by one direct
sun-to-moon control that drives the runtime theme variant.  Public pages must
not expose a separate Appearance, Night, or Paper toolbar.

The shared shell does not own route heroes, route diagrams, section order, or
route-specific content components.

Interactive material uses restrained radius, translucency, depth, and motion
to communicate affordance.  Structural boundaries such as comparison matrices
and dependency rails may remain rectilinear where straight geometry improves
reading.  Repeating square cards without semantic reason is not an accepted
site-wide layout language.

* ACCEPTED Homepage Presentation
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: homepage-presentation
:END:

The homepage is a showcase rather than an index document.  Its unique
narrative is:

1. intelligence must earn the right to act;
2. knowledge, code, scientific models, and human records enter a qualification
   core;
3. admissible action returns searchable, verifiable, and evolvable evidence;
4. human agency, open-source inspectability, and quality-data production are
   long-horizon commitments;
5. one primary exit leads into the evidence-to-operations system.

The presentation is edge-to-edge.  The host may provide runtime isolation,
but it must not frame the public site inside development gutters or expose
source-count and federation-protocol diagnostics to public readers.

The homepage may use a compact, interactive React Flow evidence circuit and a
terminal-style receipt stream.  This is a brand-level demonstration of
qualified motion: it visualizes authority moving from record to receipt.  It
does not duplicate the Platform page's lifecycle explanation or the Products
page's ecosystem inventory.

Product inventories, comparison tables, detailed lifecycle stages, value
charters, and roadmap horizons remain on their owning routes.

* ACCEPTED Route-Owned Presentation Models
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: route-owned-presentation-models
:END:

Each primary route uses a content-specific visual grammar.

** Platform

Uses an evidence lifecycle circuit.  It explains shared substrate,
qualification authority, transition ownership, and receipt return.

** Products

Uses an ecosystem constellation.  It exposes independent product ownership,
repository boundaries, and the shared evidence contract without presenting a
monolithic feature catalogue.

** Solutions

Uses a bidirectional research-to-operations bridge.  It explains how claims,
models, qualification, operation, and receipts survive institutional handoffs.

** Comparison

Uses an architectural comparison matrix.  It evaluates provenance, authority,
handoff survival, human responsibility, and learning return rather than weak
feature parity.

** Principles

Uses a manifesto and numbered charter.  Human agency, contestability,
technical equity, open-source discipline, and high-risk authority remain the
primary reading experience.

** Roadmap

Uses a dependency-ordered ladder.  Evidence states and exit signals take
precedence over arbitrary release dates or issue aggregation.

* ACCEPTED Company Footer
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: company-footer
:END:

Every public route ends with a full company-level footer.  It includes the
TAO Three K invariant, route navigation, principles and roadmap, a clear
contact surface, field notes or blog access, GitHub, source code, and the wider
open-source project directory.

The footer is a relationship and orientation surface.  It must not become a
second homepage or duplicate route-owned explanations.

* ACCEPTED Acceptance Scenarios
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: independent-route-acceptance-scenarios
:END:

- A homepage capture contains no copied product grid, comparison matrix, or
  roadmap timeline.
- Each primary route remains understandable when opened directly.
- Two different primary routes do not share the same hero and body template.
- Navigation preserves the current route without a full document reload.
- The company footer is reachable from the homepage and every primary route.
- Public captures contain no Org source counter, federation protocol label, or
  other development-only runtime status.
- The header presents a single accessible color-mode control; activating it
  changes the host =data-theme-variant= state without a duplicate toolbar.
- Homepage motion remains operable through pause and replay controls and
  honors reduced-motion preferences.
- Mobile layouts preserve the reading order and do not create horizontal page
  overflow.
