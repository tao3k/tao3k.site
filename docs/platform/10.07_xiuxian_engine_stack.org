* Architecture

Xiuxian is a deployable dual-engine product built on tao3k infrastructure.  It
is not a single workshop or a standalone workflow tool.  It can run locally,
on customer infrastructure, in hybrid environments, or as a managed cloud
service.

* Shared tao3k data interface

Apache Arrow is tao3k's common high-performance data interoperability layer.
It carries structured computation, retrieval results, workflow state,
scientific data, and evidence across language, process, deployment, and
service boundaries.

Arrow is not an engine, policy system, or evidence authority.  It defines how
structured data moves efficiently and interoperably through the platform.

* Standards at system boundaries

tao3k treats standards as durable system boundaries rather than compliance
decoration.

** Org

Org keeps structured human records, references, deep links, and linked context
addressable across research, design, and operations.

** BPMN 2.0 and DMN

BPMN 2.0 and DMN provide the Workflow and Decision Engine with established
industrial process and decision semantics.  They are a durable language for
modelling and execution, while AI may assist modelling or review without
becoming the source of workflow truth.

** Bazel, Nix, and HCL

Bazel build graphs, Nix reproducible closures, and HCL declared infrastructure
intent form a shared SDLC discipline.  Every tao3k product should use this
discipline to project one system into local, on-premises, hybrid, and cloud
deployment targets.

* Xiuxian engines

** Knowledge Engine

The Knowledge Engine searches, retrieves, indexes, and returns relevant
knowledge and evidence-aware context.

** Workflow and Decision Engine

The Workflow and Decision Engine models and executes BPMN workflows and DMN
decisions, tracks runtime state, and returns execution receipts.  BPMN and DMN
are core Xiuxian workflow and decision semantics, not merely compatibility
formats.

Both engines use the shared tao3k Arrow data interface.  Neither owns an
isolated data channel.

* Deployment

The same engine stack is deployable locally and in the cloud.  Local and cloud
are deployment surfaces of one system, not separate products.

* Product boundary

Xiuxian Workshop is the human-facing workspace within the Xiuxian product.  It
lets people search knowledge, compose workflow and decision models, run them,
inspect their state, audit outcomes, and publish governed work.

The Workshop does not replace the engines.  It gives people an intelligible,
accountable interface to them.
