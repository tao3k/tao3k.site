* Purpose

This record defines ontology as a living semantic graph. It establishes how meaning, mappings, and semantic change participate in Action Cases without requiring global ontology versions.

Ontology defines what graph elements mean. Graph algorithms compute what follows from those meanings.

* No global ontology versions

The ontology is not released as a sequence of global =v2= or =v3= packages. A global number would imply that every concept changes together and that users must migrate an entire semantic world when only one definition or mapping changed.

Instead, semantic history is preserved at the exact point where meaning changes. A new definition, relation, or mapping records what it supersedes, why it changed, who accepted it, where it applies, and when it becomes effective.

Unchanged concepts are not copied or migrated. The graph computes only the Claims, proofs, contracts, admission results, and Action Cases that actually depend on the changed semantic record.

* Semantic layers

The living ontology uses four composable layers:

| Layer | Responsibility |
|-
| Kernel Ontology | Stable primitives such as Claim, Evidence, Action, Effect, Model, Contract, Authority, Receipt, and Proof |
| Domain Ontology | Scientific, engineering, agricultural, manufacturing, software, and other domain meanings |
| Institutional Ontology | Organization-specific roles, assets, policies, responsibilities, and operational meanings |
| Action Case Vocabulary | Local concepts and mappings required by a bounded Action Case |

These are semantic scopes, not release trains. Each definition carries its own owner, provenance, applicability, and history.

* Relations have executable meaning

Relations are not arbitrary string labels.

- =supports= is not transitive by default.
- =depends-on= declares the dependency kind used by impact propagation.
- =authorized-by= does not transitively grant authority without a delegation contract.
- =derived-from= retains its source and transformation method.
- =supersedes= preserves the earlier semantic record.
- =contradicts= records a conflict but does not declare which Claim is false.

Every relation can declare domain and range, cardinality, time semantics, units, symmetry or transitivity, provenance requirements, invalidation behavior, and permitted graph algorithms.

* Exact semantic dependency

An Action Case does not bind a global ontology version. It preserves the exact semantic definitions and mappings on which its qualification depended.

If the meaning of =verified supplier= changes, the new definition becomes effective for its declared scope. The graph finds Action Cases and certificates that depended on the earlier definition. Pending actions requalify; historical actions retain the exact meaning under which they were originally authorized.

No past evidence is silently reinterpreted, and no unaffected concept is migrated.

* Ontology mappings

A mapping between research and operational concepts is a first-class semantic and evidence object. Similar names do not establish equivalence.

For example, mapping an experimental =yield= to an industrial =production throughput= must declare measurement conditions, units, statistical scope, permitted error, transformation method, approving authority, Julia computation, and Lean obligations.

Mappings can express equivalence, refinement, transformation, unit conversion, approximation, incompatibility, or deprecation. Each mapping remains contestable and revocable.

* Component boundaries

- Org and Orgize provide a human-readable surface for definitions, assumptions, mappings, and change reasons.
- ASP projects code symbols, dependencies, types, and implementation evidence into the semantic graph.
- Julia compiles semantic projections into graph structures, sparse matrices, optimization problems, and scientific models.
- Lean checks ontology constraints, mapping properties, graph-rewrite invariants, and proof obligations.
- The Evidence-Action Graph Engine selects algorithms that are valid for the declared relation semantics.
- POO Flow compiles admitted graph transformations into an executable Agent graph; Marlin applies them and records their effects at runtime.
- LLMs and agents may propose definitions or mappings but cannot silently assert them as accepted meaning.

* Human contestability

Ontology is not hidden inside embeddings or proprietary model behavior. Domain experts and affected people must be able to inspect, challenge, refine, and explain the definitions that shape an action.

Semantic authority is scoped. A technical implementation owner cannot silently redefine a social, scientific, legal, or institutional concept outside the authority granted to that owner.

* Platform statement

#+begin_quote
We do not version the whole ontology. We preserve semantic history at the exact point where meaning changes.
#+end_quote

Ontology defines meaning. Julia computes over it. Lean protects its invariants. POO Flow compiles admitted graph transformations. Marlin runs them.
