* Documentation policy

Every tracked Org document is governed by [[file:../contracts/90.01_document_contract.org][90.01 Documentation Contract]].
Claims must distinguish shipped capability, active development, research, vision,
and policy. A document must not turn a social principle into a market segment,
or present an architectural intention as shipped evidence.
