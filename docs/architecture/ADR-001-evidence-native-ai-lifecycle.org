* Context and problem statement

tao3k cannot be described accurately as an agent framework, workflow engine,
knowledge platform, coding agent, or scientific-compute product. Each label
captures one capability while hiding the system boundary that makes the
capabilities useful together.

Earlier product narratives organized the company around legacy components and a
linear knowledge-to-action chain. Those designs are replaced rather than carried
forward. They are not part of the current product or brand architecture.

* Decision

tao3k is positioned as an *evidence-native AI lifecycle ecosystem* for science
and industry.

The brand doctrine is:

#+begin_quote
Intelligence must earn the right to act.
#+end_quote

The stable company description is:

#+begin_quote
tao3k turns knowledge, code, scientific models and human records into
admissible action—and turns every action back into searchable, verifiable and
evolvable evidence.
#+end_quote

* System model

The ecosystem joins three continuous lifecycles:

1. Intelligence: record, structure, search, analyze, and validate.
2. Action: contract, admit, execute, and produce a receipt.
3. Evolution: evaluate, promote, and return trusted capability to the knowledge
   and operating system.

Contract, authority, and evidence govern every handoff. They are principles,
not three product packages.

These lifecycles are three projections over one *Admissibility Circuit*: intelligence is provisional; evidence grants qualification; execution must emit evidence; and evidence changes the next decision. The generic lifecycle and circuit are canonically defined in [[file:../platform/10.01_evidence_native_ai_lifecycle.org][Evidence-native AI Lifecycle]].

* Evidence production lifecycle
This learning-data production loop is a domain specialization of the generic Intelligence, Action, and Evolution lifecycles. It reuses the Admissibility Circuit and does not create a parallel platform lifecycle.

The Evidence-to-Action loop must also be a learning-data production loop. Capture preserves provenance and rights; qualification establishes purpose-specific admissibility; action emits contextual runtime receipts; verification preserves outcomes, counter-evidence, and failures; return publishes the versioned result to the evidence graph; reuse measures downstream value.

The resulting record is not trusted because the platform produced it. It is inspectable because the lifecycle preserves the evidence needed to evaluate provenance, authority, contract validity, reproducibility, freshness, uncertainty, rights, human accountability, contradictions, and downstream utility.

The main-site doctrine and Evidence Passport are defined in [[file:../brand/20.04_evidence_grade_learning_data.org][Evidence-grade Learning Data]]. Product-level formats and implementations remain owned by their project documentation.

* Architectural handoff

The lifecycle uses an explicit handoff rather than treating reasoning, proof, execution, and verification as one opaque agent run.

1. The Evidence-Action Graph Engine extracts a versioned Action Case from knowledge, code, models, records, contracts, and authority.
2. Julia computes graph, scientific, optimization, simulation, and numerical results together with checkable witnesses where practical.
3. Lean evaluates qualification obligations and produces a scoped admission result.
4. POO Flow compiles the admitted Agent flow; Marlin runs it and emits step-level receipts.
5. Julia and Lean verify scientific and formal postconditions.
6. Results, proofs, receipts, invalidations, and human decisions append to the graph as new evidence.

This makes qualification a pre-action decision and verification a post-action decision. Lean participates in both phases with different proof obligations; Julia supplies scientific and computational evidence; POO Flow enforces their boundary.

* Market boundary

The commercial product is designed for scientific and industrial systems. Its
primary customers are enterprises, laboratories, research institutions, and
complex engineering organizations. Technical equity and human agency are brand
commitments, not a small-team market positioning.

* Consequences

- Company copy must lead with the complete lifecycle and the evidence boundary.
- Research-to-Operations is a mission-defining continuum, not the total product
  category.
- Comparison material must use dated, evidence-backed claims.
- Human authority must be expressed as a first-class runtime capability.
- Legacy product chains must not reappear in navigation or architecture maps.
- Long-term claims must link to repositories, contracts, scenarios, benchmarks,
  or runtime receipts.
