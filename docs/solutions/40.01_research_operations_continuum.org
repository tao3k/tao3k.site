* Thesis

The gap between research and industrial operation is not only a deployment gap.
It is a break in trust, meaning, reproducibility, authority, and evidence.

Research artifacts carry assumptions, datasets, code, mathematical meaning,
uncertainty, and provenance. Industrial systems require policy, reliability,
state, recovery, deployment boundaries, accountability, and operational
evidence. Conventional handoffs often discard one side while rebuilding the
other.

* Mission

tao3k closes the trust, reproducibility, and operational gap between research
and industry.

The objective is not only to shorten deployment time. It is to preserve and
improve quality across the complete passage.

* Industry adaptation boundary

The continuum describes shared tao3k infrastructure, not a vertical product
bundle.  A laboratory, manufacturer, systems integrator, or product partner
keeps its domain model, interface, operational workflow, and accountable
decision.  tao3k carries the evidence, assumptions, qualification, authority,
durable state, recovery, and outcome record around that work.

Clinical conversation, therapeutic research, industrial intervention, and
other domain workflows are partner or customer scenarios.  They demonstrate the
constraints a vertical system must satisfy; they do not imply that tao3k sells
each scenario as a separate end-user SaaS offering.

** Solution eligibility and category axes

A Solution starts with a concrete industry or research problem, an accountable
actor, an operating environment, and a measurable outcome. It composes Platform
capabilities, tao3k Products, customer or partner components, and a deployment
profile. It does not redefine those components.

The primary Solution category is the domain system, such as Life Sciences,
Regulated Operations, or Scientific and Industrial Systems. Reusable operating
patterns, such as research-to-operations transfer or qualified Agent operation,
are secondary patterns. Local, on-premises, hybrid, and cloud are deployment
facets, not separate Solutions.

Every Solution record must therefore state: problem, user, system composition,
workflow, evidence path, authority boundary, deployment profile, intended
outcome, and what remains the responsibility of the customer or partner.

** Interactive solution navigation

The Solutions page must let a reader understand an industry system before
opening its full record. It uses one stable /Solution Preview Stage/, informed
by the direct solution previews used by Luminance, Harvey, and Tempus, rather
than decorative cards or transient tooltips.

Each trigger identifies an industry system or partner scenario. Pointer hover
and keyboard focus update the persistent stage without changing page geometry.
Click or keyboard activation pins the current preview so the reader can inspect
its links and compare details without losing context.

Every preview makes these boundaries visible:

- industry problem and accountable user;
- representative figure, workflow, or system topology;
- tao3k platform faces used by the solution;
- customer or partner capability that remains outside tao3k;
- local, on-premises, hybrid, or cloud deployment profile;
- evidence, qualification, and human-authority boundary;
- link to the complete solution record.

The preview is explanatory, not promotional decoration. A figure shows a real
system relationship, evidence path, deployment boundary, or operational
decision. It must not imply that a partner scenario is a shipped tao3k vertical
SaaS product.

*** Interaction contract

- Keep one preview surface mounted and replace its content instead of opening
  and closing adjacent floating windows.
- Use hover intent and an exit grace period so movement between neighboring
  triggers cannot produce flicker or competing panels.
- Cross-fade and translate content inside the stable surface; never animate page
  height, trigger position, or surrounding layout.
- Make keyboard focus equivalent to pointer hover. Escape releases a pinned
  preview and returns to the default selection.
- On touch devices, use tap-to-select followed by tap-to-open; do not depend on
  simulated hover.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and preserve the explanation without
  animation.
- Make the pinned state visible without covering navigation or content.

The default stage contains one strong sentence, one system figure, and a compact
capability and deployment ledger. Deeper implementation detail belongs in the
linked solution record.

* Lifecycle

#+begin_example
Research question
→ Knowledge and evidence
→ Scientific model
→ Reproducible experiment
→ Contract and assumptions
→ Validation
→ Admission
→ Governed industrial execution
→ Operational receipt
→ Outcome analysis
→ Research refinement
→ Capability promotion
#+end_example

Research and operations form a loop. Operational outcomes, failures, and
observations return as evidence rather than disappearing into logs.

* Value for research institutions

- Preserve experiment, model, source, and dataset provenance.
- Keep Julia and other domain algorithms behind explicit production contracts
  instead of rewriting away their scientific meaning.
- Compare research results with operational evidence.
- Maintain expert authority over assumptions and promotion decisions.
- Reproduce the environment, computation, and decision path.

* Value for industrial organizations

- Receive scientific capability with explicit assumptions and valid scope.
- Admit action through policy, capability, and human authority.
- Operate through typed sessions, sandbox boundaries, recovery, and replay.
- Preserve receipts for audit, analysis, and future improvement.
- Replace models, tools, and interfaces without rebuilding the entire operating
  foundation.

* Cost and quality outcomes

The continuum is designed to reduce:

- repeated implementation of the same scientific idea;
- non-reproducible research and engineering rework;
- manual translation between languages, teams, and systems;
- repeated validation caused by missing lineage;
- administrative coordination around implicit states and approvals;
- quality loss when assumptions disappear during productionization.

The intended gains are measurable improvements in reproducibility,
traceability, integration time, operational reliability, evidence coverage, and
decision confidence. Public claims must link to an actual benchmark, scenario,
or receipt before presenting a numerical result.

* Six-stage bidirectional continuum

Research-to-industry delivery is not a one-way funnel ending at deployment. It is a bidirectional evidence-preserving continuum:

1. Research Claim :: Preserve the claim, provenance, datasets, code, scientific models, assumptions, applicability boundaries, uncertainty, and known failures.
2. Reproducible Evidence :: Make the result inspectable and rerunnable outside the originating team while stating its current maturity and missing evidence.
3. Qualified Artifact :: Carry explicit permitted uses, prohibited uses, dependencies, scientific or formal obligations, human-judgment requirements, and invalidation conditions.
4. Governed Deployment :: Admit the artifact through an Action Case, qualification, and an Authority Envelope rather than allowing software packaging to imply production authority.
5. Operational Evidence :: Return performance, prediction error, drift, failure modes, human interventions, unintended effects, and the survival or failure of original assumptions.
6. Research Revision :: Use field evidence to revise models, ontology, contracts, applicability boundaries, and research questions before beginning the next cycle.

The system must carry research into operation without stripping away its evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and limits, and must carry operational reality back into research as structured evidence. Faster transfer without this continuity is not success; it merely accelerates evidence loss.

* Graph continuity

The research-to-operations continuum is represented as one evolving Evidence-Action Graph rather than a sequence of disconnected documents and deployment tickets.

An Action Case binds research claims, code, Julia models, assumptions, contracts, Lean obligations, human authority, the POO Flow compiled flow, the Marlin runtime plan, execution receipts, and post-execution verification. When an upstream assumption or evidence source changes, graph invalidation exposes the affected proofs, deployments, and operational decisions.

This continuity reduces the administrative and reproducibility gap without pretending that formal proof replaces scientific validation or that automation replaces accountable human judgment.

* Semantic and authority continuity

Research concepts do not enter operations through a global ontology upgrade. Each scientific-to-operational mapping records its meaning, conditions, units, uncertainty, transformation, evidence, and accepting authority.

An Action Case preserves the exact mappings and Evidence Set used during qualification. If one definition, assumption, or policy changes, the graph identifies the affected work without migrating unrelated knowledge or silently reinterpreting historical decisions.

Authority remains continuous as well: research approval, deployment authorization, operational responsibility, and post-deployment acceptance are distinct decisions represented in the graph.

* Production reasoning and decision calibration

The continuum needs a distinct reasoning boundary before qualification.  A
plausible model answer is not, by itself, a production decision.

#+begin_example
Question
-> GQL Rust query
-> parser-owned, knowledge, and graph evidence
-> Ascent relational deduction
-> uncertainty and applicability calibration
-> qualification, policy, and human authority
-> governed execution
-> reproducible receipt
#+end_example

GQL Rust narrows the question through precise, composable and inspectable graph
queries.  Ascent derives explicit relations from declared facts and rules.
Neither engine becomes the authority for a source, proof, policy, or action
merely by participating in the circuit.  Asserted evidence, derived relations,
uncertainty, qualification and authority remain separately inspectable.

* Message

#+begin_quote
Research should not lose its meaning in production. Operations should not lose
their evidence on the way back.
#+end_quote
