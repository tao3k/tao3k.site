* Industry adaptation

This record defines the requirements tao3k must preserve when an enterprise or
product partner builds a regulated, high-consequence, or governed AI operation
on its system layer.

The partner owns the business process, interface, user relationship, and domain
policy.  tao3k provides the source-backed context, declared conditions,
delegated authority, durable execution state, and receipt infrastructure.

* Current failure

The production gap is not merely that models can be wrong.  Enterprise systems
lose control at specific handoffs:

- relevant context is copied into a prompt without retaining source identity,
  access scope, or semantic ownership;
- a tool call has no first-class action-approval boundary, or approval failure
  silently changes the execution posture;
- checkpoint history blocks writes, forks contaminate sibling state, or an
  overlapping reconnect and cleanup operation can hang indefinitely;
- late human input, approval, or cancellation cannot be admitted exactly once
  into a durable resumed run; and
- logs describe activity but do not form a bounded, attributable record of what
  was allowed, attempted, changed, failed, and escalated.

These are state, authority, and recovery requirements.  A longer context window
or stronger model does not resolve them by itself.

* Tao3k system responsibilities

#+begin_example
Source systems and policy
-> parser-owned semantic evidence and GQL query
-> bounded Action Case
-> partner-composed POO Flow scenario and declared conditions
-> qualification, delegated authority, and human approval where required
-> Marlin runtime checkpoint, recovery, and bounded effect
-> attributable receipt and evidence return
#+end_example

Agent Semantic Protocols and GQL Rust preserve source-addressable evidence.
POO Flow expresses the case, roles, conditions, and composable workflow
structure.  Contracts, Cedar, Julia, and Lean qualify their respective
boundaries.  Marlin owns the live runtime state, recovery and effect execution.
No component turns model confidence into authority.

* Non-negotiable requirements

1. Every material retrieval retains source identity, access scope, and enough
   semantic structure to be inspected or repeated.
2. Every consequential tool invocation reaches an explicit allow, deny, defer,
   or escalation boundary before its effect.
3. Lifecycle operations have coherent ownership: they serialize, fail fast, or
   produce a typed recovery state; they do not wait indefinitely.
4. A checkpoint fork cannot alter a sibling branch's replayed history.
5. Resumed input, approval, tool completion, and receipt transitions have an
   explicit ordering and exactly-once contract.
6. A human may inspect, contest, pause, revoke, or assume responsibility at the
   defined authority boundary.

* Partner-composed scenarios

The following are examples a partner may compose.  They are not a claim that
tao3k autonomously owns an enterprise business process:

- /Source-grounded investigation/ :: assemble evidence from controlled systems
  without collapsing source identity into unowned prompt context;
- /Bounded operational handoff/ :: qualify a proposed action, named authority,
  risk class, required approval and permissible effects;
- /Interrupted work recovery/ :: preserve current state, pending input,
  approvals, executed effects and recovery conditions across pause or failure;
- /Human escalation/ :: route a decision packet to an accountable person without
  losing context, provenance, or the ability to stop the workflow; and
- /Receipt return/ :: preserve effects, failures, interventions and decisions as
  attributable material for review and later improvement.

* Evidence basis

This contract is informed by public framework issues reported after 2026-05:

- LangGraph checkpoint-history iteration blocking concurrent checkpoint writes;
- LangGraph fork updates replaying into a sibling branch;
- an AutoGen community implementation of a workbench-level tool-call approval
  gate because that boundary remains an extension seam; and
- OpenAI Agents issues concerning concurrent MCP lifecycle hangs and durable,
  exactly-once pending input for resumable state.

Those reports establish concrete implementation and operations requirements;
they do not establish that any tao3k integration is already shipped.

* Human and public boundary

The public site may describe the system requirements and partner-composable
operating path.  It MUST NOT claim that tao3k has autonomously
replaced an enterprise function, that every cross-engine integration is
production-ready, or that a customer outcome has been achieved without the
responsible public evidence record.
