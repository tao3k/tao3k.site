* Industry adaptation

This record defines the requirements tao3k must preserve when a life-sciences
company or product partner builds a therapeutic, diagnostic, laboratory, or
research workflow on its system layer.

The partner owns the domain workflow, scientific interface, and product claim.
tao3k provides the evidence, semantic, qualification, composition, and durable
execution infrastructure beneath it.

* Purpose

This is not a tao3k therapeutic-R&D product specification.  It is an industry
adaptation contract that makes the system requirements explicit without
claiming that tao3k diagnoses patients, recommends treatment, replaces a
scientist, or provides a regulatory determination.

Its narrow objective is to make research evidence, semantic relationships, and
promotion conditions inspectable before they influence a consequential research
or operational decision.

* Current failure

Drug discovery and translational research move through papers, assay results,
ELNs, protocol revisions, private datasets, CSV exports, model outputs, and
clinical evidence.  These materials can be searchable while still failing to
support a trustworthy scientific handoff.

The failure modes are concrete:

- a semantically similar text span is mistaken for a valid biological or
  clinical relationship;
- a source and target exist but their relation has no valid domain/range type;
- a projection is stale after an assay, registry, or private extension changes;
- an otherwise plausible relation has no evidence anchor, review state, or
  privacy scope; and
- an AI-generated summary crosses from candidate extraction into scientific
  assertion without an explicit promotion decision.

These are not retrieval-quality problems alone.  They are ontology, evidence,
and authority problems.

* Tao3k system responsibilities

tao3k provides an evidence-carrying ontology read-model boundary rather than a
graph store or embedding index.  A partner can compose this boundary into its
own clinical, laboratory, therapeutic, or research application.

#+begin_example
PDF / ELN / assay / dataset / protocol
-> candidate extraction
-> typed object, relation, and evidence read-model
-> domain/range, freshness, provenance, privacy, and review gate
-> ontology promotion decision
-> partner-composed POO Flow scenario
-> attributable research or operational receipt
#+end_example

The source systems retain authority over their records.  Wendao materialises a
typed read-model with source identity, evidence spans, projection version and
status.  GQL Rust can ask precise questions over the graph-shaped evidence.
Ascent can derive declared relations without converting a derivation into source
authority.  Julia and Catlab/ACSets remain validation and research-proof
surfaces for structural invariants; they do not become the source parser,
ontology mutation authority, or resident runtime.

* Non-negotiable requirements

No material relation enters a high-confidence research evidence set unless it
has all of the following:

1. a registered object type and relation kind;
2. source and target objects whose types satisfy the declared domain/range;
3. an addressable evidence anchor and source identity;
4. a current projection version, explicit freshness or staleness status, and
   applicable privacy scope;
5. a confidence and human-review state that distinguish candidate from accepted
   evidence; and
6. a named promotion authority for the intended research use.

An invalid relation is valuable output.  A missing type, stale projection, or
missing evidence anchor becomes a visible gate failure, not a silently ranked
answer.

* Partner-composed scenarios

The following are examples a partner may compose.  They are not a universal
medical workflow or a claim that tao3k supplies an end-user clinical product:

- /Evidence Intake/ :: classify incoming literature, assay and protocol material
  into candidate objects, relations, and evidence anchors;
- /Relation Readiness/ :: test domain/range, registry presence, projection
  freshness, privacy, and review conditions before promotion;
- /Hypothesis Review/ :: assemble a bounded evidence case for an accountable
  scientist to inspect, contest, accept, defer, or reject; and
- /Evidence Return/ :: attach outcomes, failed assumptions, corrections, and
  new evidence to the originating case.

Profiles compose these scenarios according to a research program, therapeutic
area, and institutional authority.  The composition is not a claim that all
biopharma programmes share one ontology or one approval process.

* Requirements extracted from the Wendao ontology review

The current Solution requirements follow the *Wendao Episteme / Catlab-ACSets
Forward Report* (2026-06-26):

- establish a typed read-model contract before considering Catlab/ACSets as a
  runtime dependency;
- keep Rust/RDF materialisation and source validation separate from Julia/Catlab
  structural validation;
- preserve provenance, source span, parser strategy, confidence, review state,
  evidence status, projection version, freshness, and privacy scope with each
  research fact;
- require domain/range readiness before high-confidence search evidence or
  ontology promotion; and
- model Catlab/ACSets and satisfiability tools as isolated proof backends until
  typed handoff, versioning, performance, replay, and audit evidence justify a
  narrower production boundary.

* Human and public boundary

The public site may describe the system requirements and the partner-composable
scenarios.  It MUST NOT claim clinical validation, regulatory approval, a
deployed therapeutic programme, improved patient outcomes, or a numerical
discovery-performance result without the responsible evidence record.
