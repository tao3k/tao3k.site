* Position

Advanced technical capability should broaden participation across society, not
concentrate power in fewer institutions.

Technical equity is a company principle and social commitment. It is not the
commercial category of the tao3k platform and must not be used to position the
product as a small-team or student utility.

* Human-paced transition

#+begin_quote
AI can move at machine speed. People deserve to grow at human speed.
#+end_quote

The cost of rapid technical transition must not be pushed entirely onto people
with the least institutional power. A responsible transition provides time,
explanation, simulation, staged autonomy, reversible choices, and a path to new
forms of contribution.

* Students

Students need more than generated answers. They need an environment in which
they can move through a capability ladder:

#+begin_example
Ask → Understand → Reproduce → Modify → Validate → Build → Contribute
#+end_example

AI should lower the barrier to complex knowledge, code, and scientific tools
without hiding how conclusions were formed.

* Farmers and field practitioners

Farmers and field practitioners hold local, environmental, temporal, and tacit
knowledge that a general model cannot assume. AI should connect scientific
research, observations, operational data, and market information while keeping
the practitioner's experience visible, contestable, and valuable.

The goal is not to let an opaque recommendation erase local judgment. The goal
is to make scientific and field evidence useful together.

* Technical workers and operators

Technicians and operators understand equipment, failure, maintenance, safety,
and real operating constraints. AI should help them diagnose systems, anticipate
risk, access knowledge, and turn field experience into reusable organizational
evidence.

Automation should create a path from repetitive execution toward diagnosis,
supervision, craft, and system improvement.

* Participation during rapid technical transition

Students, farmers, technical workers, independent builders, and people with limited institutional resources are not a narrow tao3k market segment. They are a test of whether the ecosystem's values survive contact with unequal access to capital, compute, education, and organizational power.

tao3k does not promise to preserve every existing job form or deny that automation changes work. It does reject workforce removal as the only measure of AI value, people as obsolete inefficiencies, and technical speed as a justification for denying participation. The ecosystem should provide inspectable conclusions, open formats, data portability, local and self-hosted foundations, learnable documentation, attribution, and realistic paths for people to acquire new capability at a humanly sustainable pace.

Technical equity means expanding who can learn, inspect, contest, build, migrate, and create value with advanced systems. It does not mean hiding complexity, lowering evidence standards, or turning a public-value commitment into a marketing category.

* Design implications

- Preserve sources, assumptions, and uncertainty instead of returning a bare
  recommendation.
- Support observe, suggest, confirm, and execute modes rather than forcing an
  immediate jump to full autonomy.
- Provide sandbox and simulation paths for learning and transition.
- Preserve domain language and local knowledge through structured records and
  ontology mappings.
- Keep consequential actions contestable and reversible where the domain
  permits.
- Avoid model and platform lock-in that removes a person's accumulated work.
- Measure whether users gain lasting capability, not only task throughput.

* Evidence-grade learning data

Technical equity includes the right to become a recognized producer of knowledge, not merely a consumer of AI output. People and communities who contribute records, corrections, observations, domain practice, or operational outcomes must be able to retain attribution, applicable rights, context, and a visible relationship to the value created from their work.

Quality requirements must remain understandable and attainable. tao3k should help contributors produce contract-checked, provenance-bearing, reproducible evidence without requiring access to a hyperscale laboratory. This does not waive privacy, consent, safety, or domain qualification requirements.

The canonical lifecycle and quality dimensions are defined in [[file:../brand/20.04_evidence_grade_learning_data.org][Evidence-grade Learning Data]].

* Boundary with commercial positioning

The commercial platform remains designed for scientific and industrial
institutions. The same foundations can lower barriers for students, farmers,
independent practitioners, and technical workers, but those communities belong
to the public-value commitment rather than the product-category headline.

* Inspectable meaning and authority

Technical equity requires more than access to an AI interface. People must be able to inspect the Claims, evidence, assumptions, semantic definitions, and authority boundaries that shape consequential decisions.

Ontology cannot remain hidden inside proprietary embeddings. Domain experts, students, workers, farmers, researchers, and affected communities must be able to challenge a definition or mapping without first becoming model engineers.

Likewise, machine qualification cannot silently replace human standing. Authority Envelopes make the permitted scope, responsible people, affected interests, and revocation conditions visible.
