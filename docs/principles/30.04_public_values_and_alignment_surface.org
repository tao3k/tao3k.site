* ACCEPTED Purpose
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: public-values-alignment-purpose
:END:

The public Principles route is tao3k's company-values surface. It helps people,
communities, collaborators, and institutions understand what tao3k believes
about human capability, knowledge, AI, and responsible action.

The route is not a product-benefit list, an audience-qualification screen, or a technical
governance manual. It expresses the beliefs that shape what tao3k builds, how it
works, and the future it intends to strengthen.

* ACCEPTED Shared horizon
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: public-values-shared-horizon
:END:

tao3k is building foundational technology, an open community ecosystem, and
trusted institutional infrastructure. The project is designed to compound human
capability over a long horizon rather than follow a short-lived AI,
entertainment, or market cycle.

People are the primary source of durable value. Technology should help their
knowledge travel farther, their judgment become more informed, and their ability
to create responsible action grow over time.

* ACCEPTED People are the compounding asset
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: public-values-people-compounding-asset
:END:

Models, interfaces, and markets will change. The durable asset is people: their
domain knowledge, judgment, craft, relationships, and capacity to learn. tao3k
builds systems that strengthen those capabilities rather than treat people as
replaceable inputs.

The public route MUST make three commitments legible:

- AI should extend human capability rather than make human contribution
  invisible;
- communities and practitioners should remain recognized producers of knowledge
  and value;
- each cycle of work should leave people and institutions with more durable
  capability than they had before.

* ACCEPTED Enterprise partners
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: public-values-enterprise-partners
:END:

Institutional collaboration matters when it strengthens the durable capability
of the people who research, build, operate, maintain, and govern consequential
systems. Real operating contexts should connect domain knowledge to responsible
action without making practitioners invisible or dependent on opaque control.

The public route MUST make three commitments legible:

- collaboration should leave people and institutions with more knowledge,
  judgment, and operating capability than they had before;
- the knowledge-action interface should keep evidence, responsibility, and human
  authority visible from research through operation;
- outcomes, failures, and lessons should return as reusable evidence for the
  wider ecosystem, subject to privacy, consent, and applicable rights.

* ACCEPTED Knowledge equality and the capability gap
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: public-values-knowledge-equality-capability-gap
:END:

AI can make information abundant without making people equally capable of using
it. Knowledge equality therefore requires more than access to answers. People
need a realistic path to understand evidence, challenge meaning, contribute
knowledge, and turn what they know into action.

As information asymmetry contracts, the decisive inequality shifts toward
capability: who can frame useful questions, judge evidence, work effectively
with AI, organize action, and learn from outcomes. tao3k builds to narrow that
gap while preserving expertise, responsibility, and human authority.

* ACCEPTED The knowledge-action interface
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: public-values-knowledge-action-interface
:END:

The next human--AI interface is not merely a better conversation with a model.
It must help people move from access to understanding, from understanding to
composition, and from composition to qualified action while keeping evidence,
context, authorship, and responsibility attached.

tao3k expresses this interface as one composable capability cycle. The Scheme
composition owns the executable topology. The Org sections below own its public
meaning and join the Bundle v1 nodes by their semantic symbol, never by a
TypeScript-generated plan or a positional guess.

* ACCEPTED Compounding model
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: public-values-compounding-model
:END:

The route presents the shared horizon as one compounding sequence:

1. people contribute purpose, judgment, craft, and domain knowledge;
2. open knowledge and AI reduce barriers to information and technical tools;
3. the knowledge-action interface turns understanding into qualified action;
4. outcomes return as evidence that people and communities can inspect and
   reuse;
5. each cycle increases durable human and institutional capability.

* ACCEPTED Not our horizon
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: public-values-not-our-horizon
:END:

tao3k does not trade human capability for a faster technology story. It does not
seek:

- automation that treats people as obsolete inefficiencies;
- abundant answers without a path to understanding, judgment, or action;
- opaque systems that erase knowledge producers or silently transfer authority;
- growth that extracts from communities without strengthening their capability;
- technical speed that weakens evidence, responsibility, or the right to
  contest.

* ACCEPTED Visual doctrine: cyber-industrial evidence lab
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: public-values-cyber-industrial-visual-doctrine
:END:

The public Principles route presents tao3k as a cyber-industrial evidence
lab: a place where future infrastructure is built, inspected, maintained, and
made answerable to people. It MUST NOT imitate generic corporate consulting,
decorative terminal nostalgia, or dystopian cyberpunk spectacle.

The visual system combines three layers:

- *future infrastructure* provides architectural depth, ecological continuity,
  and a visible long horizon;
- *computational substrate* exposes evidence graphs, qualification boundaries,
  provenance, and inspectable system state;
- *operational runtime* shows people as researchers, builders, maintainers, and
  legitimate authority holders rather than as disposable inputs.

The route calls this operating surface the *Trust Kernel*. Its primary statement
is: "Intelligence must earn the right to act." Trust is represented as an
architecture with observable invariants, not as a brand promise.

The night presentation uses graphite, petrol teal, electric cyan, oxidized
coral, restrained amber, and bone white. Image and motion may establish scale
and depth, but they MUST preserve copy legibility, reduced-motion behavior, and
fast first rendering. Generated visual assets MUST be original to tao3k,
contain no embedded text or logos, and remain subordinate to Org-authored
meaning.

The pressure-test interaction is presented as a live Org contract. It MUST
preserve stable geometry while a user explores choices and MUST reveal the
applicable decision boundary without hover jitter or decorative motion.

The hero MAY use a bounded WebGL fragment shader when it materially improves the
scene. Motion MUST remain attached to material already depicted in the source
image. The initial sample detects the existing bridge and planter foliage by
image region and color, then applies independent low-amplitude, multi-phase
displacement fields only to those pixels. The planter uses two elliptical
signed-distance masks, stricter green-material classification, explicit human
exclusion by geometry, and a nonlinear root anchor. Low-frequency value noise
controls branch bending while a second high-frequency field controls leaf-tip
flutter; vertical displacement remains near zero. One restrained guidance
signal runs along the distant rail. It MUST NOT add a vehicle, chamber
animation, free-floating object, or unrelated technological overlay.

Global brightness pulsing, decorative network lines, floating spheres, and
technology overlays that do not arise from a depicted object are prohibited.
Touch input, background tabs, and reduced-motion preferences MUST receive a
static or reduced presentation. A WebGL layer MAY run entirely in the client
when it is isolated in an asynchronous chunk, limited in device-pixel ratio,
paused outside the viewport, and backed by an immediate static image. Autoplay
video and continuous layout animation are outside this route's performance
budget.

** ACCEPTED Trust Kernel structural cutaway
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: public-values-trust-kernel-structural-cutaway
:END:

After the primary values narrative, the route MUST offer the statement "Trust is
an architecture, not a promise" as secondary proof. The structural cutaway
shows how the company values become operating constraints rather than replacing
them with a technical opening. The operating position and cutaway share one
full-width surface, with stable geometry at every interaction state.

The cutaway contains four semantic layers:

1. *Evidence substrate* preserves provenance, context, assumptions, limits,
   supporting evidence, and unresolved dissent.
2. *Qualification boundary* separates capability from permission through
   applicable evidence, policy, and proof.
3. *Human authority* preserves attributable power to inspect, contest, pause,
   appeal, and revoke consequential action.
4. *Action to receipt* returns outcomes, decisions, and failures to searchable
   and contestable evidence.

The active layer MAY change through explicit pointer or keyboard selection, but
the detail viewport MUST retain fixed dimensions. Human authority uses
restrained amber; evidence flow uses cyan. These colors encode system semantics
and MUST NOT become general decoration. Layer selection MUST NOT trigger layout
movement, hover expansion, automatic authority activation, or blinking state.

The return path MAY animate as a single bounded signal from operational outcome
to evidence. Reduced-motion presentation MUST show a complete static cutaway.
The compact "TRUST IS NOT A SKIN" mark is a material stamp, not an independent
content column.

* ACCEPTED Presentation contract
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: public-values-alignment-presentation-contract
:END:

The route MUST provide, in narrative-first and proof-second order:

- a clear statement that people are the primary compounding asset;
- a definition of knowledge equality that includes access, understanding,
  contest, contribution, and application;
- an explanation of the capability gap that emerges as information asymmetry
  contracts;
- a positive account of the human--AI knowledge-action interface;
- a compounding sequence from people and knowledge through action and returned
  evidence;
- a clear statement of the future tao3k refuses to build;
- the Trust Kernel structural cutaway as secondary proof of the operating
  commitments.

The public principle ledger MUST make these seven boundaries explicit:

1. people, their knowledge, and their capacity to learn are the primary
   compounding asset;
2. knowledge equality requires the ability to understand, contest, contribute,
   and apply knowledge, not only receive answers;
3. reduced information asymmetry must not become a new capability monopoly;
4. the knowledge-action interface preserves human purpose, judgment,
   responsibility, and the right to stop;
5. evidence precedes model confidence and consequential action;
6. outcomes return as inspectable evidence that strengthens future capability;
7. open foundations require durable community and institutional stewardship.

The route MUST NOT reduce the Principles page to audience qualification, a
technical governance checklist, automation spectacle, or unsupported brand
slogans.
